# Throttle-probe gate (fauxcasa-ncv) — does Qt QWidget raster throttle to vsync on this compositor?
# 2026-06-13 GNOME/Mutter Wayland, AMD Radeon 780M, eDP-1 @165Hz
# A vsync-throttled surface would pin paint_hz at ~165 regardless of timer; it does not.

verdict: inconclusive: timer (60Hz) <= refresh; paint_hz ≈ timer just means paints follow the driver. Re-run with --hz > 165 to decide throttling.
