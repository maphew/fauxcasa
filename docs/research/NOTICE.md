# Research Archive Notice

The research archive contains source-attributed reference material used to
understand Picasa behavior, file formats, workflows, and prior art.

Unless a specific file says otherwise, archived third-party material in this
directory is preserved with attribution for research, interoperability,
historical, and link-rot-resistance purposes. Fauxcasa does not claim copyright
ownership over that third-party material and does not relicense it under
Fauxcasa's project licenses.

Original notes, summaries, indexes, and commentary written specifically for
Fauxcasa are licensed under `CC-BY-SA-4.0`, except where they quote, summarize,
or incorporate third-party material whose own terms continue to apply.

Large or copyright-sensitive artifacts such as videos, extracted stills,
installers, and binary dumps should remain out of git unless there is a clear
legal and project reason to commit them. Prefer gitignored cache paths with
provenance, hashes, and rebuild instructions.
