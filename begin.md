# Fauxcasa

I loved Picasa photo management program. It's been years since google killed it. I read it was still making $8 million a year, even though it was primarily a free program (advanced web albums were cost plus), when google decided it wasn't aligned with their goals. These agentic AI programs are really smart I hear. Let's find out if they can reverse engineer and rebuild the best of what Picasa was.
